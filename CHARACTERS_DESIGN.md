# CHARACTER DESIGN BIBLE
## 10 Unique Characters - Skills, Lore, Visual Identity

---

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

- **Uniqueness**: Each character distinct personality + playstyle
- **Skill Synergy**: Active skill + passive trait complement each other
- **Visual Clarity**: Easy to identify by silhouette + color
- **Lore Depth**: 2-3 sentence backstory (world-building for Season 1+)

## VISUAL IDENTITY CHECKLIST

For 3D generation, ensure each character has:

- ✅ **Unique silhouette** (instantly recognizable)
- ✅ **Color palette** (2-3 dominant colors, 1-2 accents)
- ✅ **Personality** (pose, expression reflects character)
- ✅ **Equipment** (thematic gear/weapons/accessories)
- ✅ **Glow/Aura** (visual element that stands out)
- ✅ **Asymmetry** (not perfectly symmetrical — more interesting)

---

## SCENARIO.GG GENERATION NOTES

**Critical for 3D quality**:
- Always add: "highly detailed 3D cel-shaded model, toon style, no glossy PBR"
- Specify pose (standing/running/action, not T-pose)
- Include personality keywords (determined/mysterious/powerful/energetic)
- Mention equipment (armor/weapons/accessories)

**Do NOT generate**:
- Weapons only (need full character)
- Multiple characters in one image (1 per GLB)
- Generic "character design" (specify exactly who)
